# AGER Office — recurring operational evidence service

Commercial service operated by wolf-systems. The open AGER specification remains independent from this service.

## Core promise

Keep every covered AI-agent deployment represented by a current, reviewable operational evidence record instead of producing one-off compliance documents.

## Included per covered deployment

- maintained AGER record;
- permission/access map;
- autonomy and Blast Radius review;
- human-control and escalation evidence;
- change-event maintenance;
- evidence-reference integrity checks;
- incident/near-miss record preparation;
- quarterly evidence review;
- export package for customer/internal review.

## Service levels for first commercial conversion

### AGER Office Starter
- up to 3 active deployments;
- monthly evidence refresh;
- one quarterly review;
- email support;
- EUR 149/month, net.

### AGER Office Partner
- up to 10 active deployments;
- monthly evidence refresh;
- monthly change review;
- quarterly governance review;
- partner/customer hand-off packages;
- EUR 399/month, net.

### Additional deployment
EUR 29/month, net, unless a separate volume agreement applies.

Pricing is an initial commercial hypothesis for founding customers and can be replaced by a negotiated partner rate before acceptance.

## Founding-customer conversion

For a successful free 1-agent pilot:
- no setup fee for the first three paid deployments if converted within 30 days of pilot review;
- first month cancellable at month-end;
- no certification or legal-advice promise;
- customer remains responsible for legal classification and final compliance decisions.

## Operating model

Customer or implementation partner provides deployment changes and evidence references. wolf-systems maintains the AGER layer, flags missing evidence and produces review-ready records. Secrets and unnecessary personal data are excluded by default.
