# AGER Partner-Led Go-to-Market

Updated: 2026-08-14

## Core commercial thesis

AGER should be sold primarily through AI-agent platforms, implementation partners, system integrators and BPOs rather than as a generic compliance product for individual SMEs.

The repeatable commercial unit is not one customer = one record. The target model is:

`AI-agent platform -> implementation/technology partner -> many customer deployments -> recurring AGER evidence records`

AGER is positioned as an operational governance/evidence layer for productive AI agents: identity, permissions, autonomy, human controls, change history, evidence, incidents and exportable records.

Do not lead with certification claims or legal conclusions. AGER is not an official compliance certificate and does not replace legal advice.

## Three commercial tiers

### Tier A — Founding Pilot

Targets: telli, dalvon, VoiceBooker, botario, Synthflow.

Offer:
- one productive agent;
- free AGER Exposure Baseline;
- agent inventory/identity;
- tool/API/MCP permission map;
- autonomy level;
- blast-radius score;
- human-control/kill-switch review;
- change/evidence baseline;
- incident-readiness package.

Conversion objective:
- recurring Governance & Evidence Office;
- per-deployment or base + per-agent commercial model;
- optional white-label delivery to the platform/partner's own customers.

### Tier B — Multipliers

Targets: sipgate, VIER.

Offer:
- demonstrate AGER on one internal or partner deployment;
- then evaluate AGER as a standard deployment hand-off/evidence artifact for partner ecosystems.

Commercial objective:
- recurring records across many customer deployments;
- partner/reseller route rather than single end-customer sale.

### Tier C — Strategic Technology Partnerships

Targets: Parloa, NiCE Cognigy, Spitch.

Do not pitch as a small compliance consultancy.

Position AGER as:
- complementary technology;
- interoperable evidence layer;
- governance hand-off format;
- deployment inventory and permission record;
- optional export/adapter for partner/customer deployments.

Commercial objective:
- technology partnership;
- integration/adapter;
- co-delivery or ecosystem listing;
- long-term multiplier effect.

## Message architecture

Primary positioning:

> You already run AI agents in production. AGER gives each deployment a machine-readable record of identity, permissions, autonomy, human controls, changes and operational evidence.

Pilot CTA:

> Give us one productive agent. We create its AGER Exposure Baseline at no cost and show whether the artifact can scale across your customer or partner deployments.

Strategic CTA:

> We would like to evaluate AGER as a complementary governance/evidence layer for your technology and partner ecosystem.

## Channel rules

1. Prefer explicit partner application, partner form, demo/consultation route, or an existing conversation thread.
2. `partner@wolf-systems.online` is the default sender for new partner applications.
3. `andreas@wolf-systems.online` is used for warm replies, technical follow-up and pilot discussions.
4. `kontakt@wolf-systems.online` remains the general contact mailbox.
5. Do not execute unsolicited automated voice calls.
6. CALL-E is used only after explicit callback/telephone consent or a booked call.
7. Record the evidence for consent and run `scripts/call_gate.py` before an automated call.
8. The AI caller identifies itself as an AI-assisted caller and names wolf-systems at the start of the call.
9. No recording without separate permission.
10. Respect opt-outs immediately.

## Success metrics

Track:
- partner applications submitted;
- positive reply rate;
- technical meetings booked;
- free pilots started;
- pilots converted to paid recurring service;
- average deployments per partner;
- projected MRR per partner;
- time from first partner contact to first AGER record.

## Product implications

The sales strategy should drive the product roadmap toward:
- reusable deployment templates;
- platform adapters;
- partner/customer namespaces;
- white-label evidence packs;
- bulk agent inventory/import;
- recurring change/evidence refresh;
- SAFE-compatible export where technically appropriate and without claiming official endorsement;
- API-first operation so AGER can sit between a platform and its customer deployment process.
