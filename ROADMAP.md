## Commercial implementation track

The open specification is independent from commercial services. Potential implementations include:

- external AI governance office
- agent inventory service
- permission review
- deployment-change monitoring
- transparency evidence monitoring
- incident evidence preparation
- white-label governance services for AI agencies and MSPs

## v1.0 objective

A stable, vendor-neutral operational evidence format that can be implemented without purchasing a proprietary governance platform.
